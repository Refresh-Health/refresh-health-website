Refresh health HealthOS Platform

A well-architected, secure, and HIPAA-compliant platform that incorporates FHIR, Smart on FHIR, medical NLP, and hyperautomation to integrate data from multiple sources and deliver actionable insights at the point of care

Platform Overview

The HealthOS platform leverages population-based analytics to provide insights into patient health at the population level. This enables providers to identify trends, risk factors, and opportunities for intervention that can improve health outcomes for their patients.
