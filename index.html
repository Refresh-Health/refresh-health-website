Our Approach

Elevating patient care through the seamless integration of compassionate individuals, innovative platforms, and efficient processes.

Platform

Aggregate, Organize & Reduce

Refresh health HealthOS consolidates data from various sources such as EHR, claims, SDoH, remote devices, organizes and deduplicates to create a meaningful summary of the patient's healthcare journey.

Transform, Interpret & Synthesize

Refresh health HealthOS leverages data transformation into codable, empowering evidence-based decision support at multiple touchpoints within the care delivery process. This enables healthcare professionals to access valuable insights and guidance at the point of care, enhancing the quality and effectiveness of patient care and charge capture.

Process

Disease Specific Care Pathways

Disease-specific care pathways that incorporate evidence-based integrative medicine offer a holistic approach to managing and treating conditions, combining the best of conventional medicine with complementary and alternative therapies for improved patient outcomes.

Caring Beyond the Four Walls

Refresh health HealthOS and the care team deliver compassionate and personalized care to patients in their homes, communities, or remote areas. By extending care beyond the confines of hospitals and clinics, healthcare professionals can meet patients where they are and address their specific needs.
