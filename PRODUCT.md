## Platform

## Users

**Primary (equal weight):**
- **Health system executives** — CFO, CMO, VP Clinical Operations evaluating enterprise care management platform deals. Arriving with procurement context: seeking proof of clinical credibility, compliance pedigree, and measurable ROI.
- **Physician practice owners / group leaders** — Independent practices and multi-specialty groups looking for a care management partner that handles both the technology and the care team burden. Arriving with operational pain: provider burnout, revenue leakage from unreimbursed chronic care work, and fragmented patient data.

## Product Purpose

Refresh Health is an AI-native connected healthcare platform designed by physicians to improve patient outcomes, increase provider revenue, and decrease provider burnout. It unifies patient data from EHR, claims, SDoH, and remote devices into a single complete record, then delivers actionable clinical decision support at the point of care. Founded and operated by physicians, it pairs the HealthOS technology platform with a physician-led care team — offering software and human care delivery as a bundled service, not separate products.

## Positioning

Primary tagline: **"Connecting Care, Improving Outcomes."** The platform is framed as AI-native and physician-designed, with the three-outcome promise (better patient outcomes, more revenue, less burnout) as the core value proposition. The competitive differentiator — combining deep AI + FHIR data unification with a physician-led clinical care team as a single bundle — is the underlying proof, surfaced through the platform and solutions pages rather than in the hero.

## Operating Context

Both buyer types arrive at the marketing site during evaluation or discovery, not during active clinical use. Health system executives may be in formal RFP cycles; practice owners often arrive via referral or conference. Both need proof of: (1) clinical credibility (physician-founded, physician-led), (2) technical trust (HIPAA-compliant, FHIR-based, enterprise-grade), and (3) financial return (reimbursable programs, revenue cycle management). The care delivery context itself — RPM, CCM, Hospital @ Home — happens outside this website; the site's job is to earn the conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Platform (HealthOS):**
- HIPAA-compliant; built on FHIR, Smart on FHIR, medical NLP, and hyperautomation
- Aggregates and deduplicates data from EHR, claims, SDoH, remote devices into a unified patient record
- Population-management analytics: identify trends, risk factors, and intervention opportunities at scale
- Patient 360: complete view of patient health; personalized care plans and targeted interventions
- Workflow-enabled system: customizable clinical workflows for care management

**Solutions:**
- Disease Management Programs: Diabetes (Type 1 & 2), Pre-Diabetes, Heart Failure, COPD, Hypertension
- Reimbursable Virtual Care: RPM (99453, 99454), CCM (99490, 99491), TCM, RTM (98975, 98977), PCM (99424, 99425), AWV (G0438, G0439)
- Hospital @ Home: hospital-level care delivered to older adults at home
- Ambient Listening Virtual Scribes
- End-to-End Revenue Cycle Management
- Device Lifecycle Management

**Constraints:**
- HIPAA and compliance messaging must remain accurate and prominent across all surfaces
- CPT/HCPCS billing codes listed on the Solutions page are real and must not be altered without clinical review

## Product Principles

1. **Clinical credibility is the foundation** — physician-founded and physician-led is not a tagline; it is the primary trust signal for both buyer types and must anchor every major surface.
2. **One complete record before anything else** — the value chain starts with unified patient data; all downstream capabilities (decision support, care pathways, billing) depend on it.
3. **Care that extends beyond the encounter** — the product's reach into homes, communities, and remote settings is the concrete proof of "connected care"; surfaces should show it, not just claim it.
4. **Revenue and outcomes are aligned** — the reimbursable program suite makes better chronic care financially self-sustaining for providers; this alignment is a differentiator worth making explicit.
5. **Technology recedes, clinicians lead** — HealthOS serves physicians rather than replacing their judgment; the human care team is co-equal to the platform in the product story.
